# Briefing: "Elysius" Minecraft modpack (state as of 2026-10-14)

I'm building a Minecraft modpack together with Claude Code, which edits the files on my PC. In this chat I
want to **brainstorm ideas** (story, quests, bosses, NPC dialogue, balance). Afterwards I take the results
back to Claude Code to implement. Please keep suggestions within the technical limits listed at the end.

## Basics
- Pack icon made by **elisa/sultan** (please credit her when it comes up).
- Minecraft **1.20.1**, **Forge 47.4.10**, Java 17, Prism Launcher, 6.4 GB RAM. Played with friends
  (hosted via Essential).
- Theme: **elytra flight** (everyone starts with an unbreakable elytra), **TaCZ guns**, boss dungeons,
  roaming zombie hordes, **FTB Quests**. Story chapters are written in **German**.
- About 55 mods, among them: TaCZ (53 guns, 24 calibres, 95 attachments), The Aether, Alex's Mobs,
  Terralith + Tectonic, When Dungeons Arise, Repurposed Structures (trimmed), YUNG's structure mods,
  Zombie Awareness, Traveler's Backpack, Elytra Slot, Jade + Neat (health bars), AmbientSounds,
  Entity Texture Features (texture swaps by mob name), Pehkui (mob scaling), Paxi (global datapacks),
  Chunky, Xaero's maps, JEI, Veinminer.
- No KubeJS. Custom content is made with datapacks and resource packs, generated by Python scripts.

## What exists so far

### Economy and quests (FTB Quests, 26 chapters, about 300 quests)
- **Shop chapters:** guns (gold / diamond / emerald tiers), ammo (a single trade plus a one-click full
  stack; e.g. 1 copper = 5 rounds of 9mm), attachments, rockets. Shop quests show no popups.
- Bounty chapters per dimension (Overworld, Nether, Aether, End), "Taking Flight", Good Deeds,
  Long-Term Goals, Backpack Upgrades.
- **"Boss Dungeons" chapter**: one quest per boss (find the structure + kill the boss → gun + ammo).
- **"Horde Leaders" chapter**: one quest per leader type plus "kill all six".
- Structure chests in every dimension contain guns, attachments and ammo in 5 difficulty tiers.

### Story chapter "Zwischen den Zeiten" (being rebuilt from scratch)
Idea: the story splits into an **ancient path** (iron, Aether, swords) and a **modern path** (copper,
TaCZ guns). At some points both paths **meet**. Quests stay **invisible until their predecessor is done**.
Text goes into quest descriptions, not into books.

| # | Quest | Task | Reward | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Eisern aber weich** (ancient start) | smelt your first iron | Aether golden oak sapling | "In alten Zeiten kämpfte man unerbittlich mit Stahl gegen die Dunkelheit des Nethers, doch nur der goldene Aether hielt die Ungeheuer der Nacht fern." |
| 2 | **Kupferpfeil** (modern start) | have a copper ingot | 1× 9mm round | "Fun Fact: Kupfer ist tatsächlich antibakteriell, ob das auch für die fliegenden Kugeln gilt?" |
| 3 | **Schwerter und Kugeln** (paths meet, needs 1 + 2) | craft **any sword + 9mm round** into a **"Kackhaufen"** (a placeable poop head, decoration) | hidden: **Der Bote appears** | subtitle: "Was passiert, wenn ich beides kombiniere?"; text after completion: "Das war ein Griff ins Klo - und wer ist dieser komische Vogel?" |

### NPC "Der Bote" (inspired by the messenger in the book *Erebos*)
A wandering trader on a black horse who appears after quest 3. He can't be hurt and never despawns.
His lines appear in chat with a sound, and the answers are clickable buttons.
1. "Seid gegrüßt. Ein Bad würde euch guttun."
2. "Ihr seid <player name>, richtig?" [JA] [NEIN]
   - NEIN (a lie): 1 heart of damage, blindness, a roll of thunder, **his eyes turn red**, then
     "Ich warne euch, lügt mich nicht an, es könnte euch alles kosten. Ich frage euch - Ihr heißt <name>?"
     with only [JA]. That lifts the blindness and the red eyes.
3. "Alleine schafft ihr es keinen Tag zu überleben. Ich biete euch einen Deal an - arbeitet für mich."
   [JA] [NEIN]
   - NEIN: blind, "Lasst mich euch zeigen wo euer Schicksal liegt ohne mich." → teleported into a
     **vision**: a black void, standing **frozen on a single cracked deepslate block**. "Seht ihr?" with only
     [JA] → back to where you stood, the deal counts as accepted.
4. **OPEN: what happens after the deal is accepted?** Currently a placeholder line ("Gut. Dann zeigt mir,
   was ihr begehrt.") and his trades unlock. The trades are placeholders too (Kackhaufen → diamond,
   rotten flesh → rockets, copper → 9mm).
5. After each trade: "Ist das alles was euer Herz begehrt?" JA = he gallops away and vanishes in smoke;
   NEIN = he stays.
- Ambient sounds go quiet near him and in the custom dimensions.

### Boss dungeons (datapack, reskinned vanilla mobs)
- **Ashen Barrow** (badlands, desert and volcanic biomes): a ruin with a shaft down to a throne hall.
  **Ashen Warlord** (wither skeleton, 300 HP, netherite gear), 3 phases, guard waves, a fire burst.
- **Tempest Crown** (a floating sky arena at about y=245, elytra only, rocket refill barrel):
  **Tempest Herald** (custom storm-ghast model). Only the **ruby on its forehead** takes real damage;
  lightning in phase 2; Storm Rider phantoms.
- **The Hollow** (its own void dimension, entered through crying-obsidian **Rift Gates**):
  **Hollow Magus** (a 2× illusioner with a purple skin), invulnerable while 4 end crystals stand; shoot
  them to break his shield; vex waves; a void pulse throws you into the air.

### Zombie hordes and leaders
- At night or in thunderstorms, roughly every 27 minutes outdoors: a horde of 8–16 zombies (husks or
  drowned depending on biome). Gunfire attracts zombies.
- Every horde has **one leader** (with its own skin, glow colour and bossbar); kill it and the horde
  scatters. The names come from a friend's design:
  **Läufer** (sprints; counter: shotgun), **Brecher** (armoured tank; armour-piercing ammo and headshots),
  **Spucker** (throws poison; sniper), **Schreier** (screams to call zombies; suppressor), **Kriecher**
  (invisible in the dark, grabs your legs; light), **Platzer** (explodes on death; keep your distance).

## Technical limits (what's possible without writing a Java mod)
- **No genuinely new items, blocks or mobs.** Workarounds: player heads with custom textures (placeable),
  vanilla mobs with NBT, Pehkui scaling and ETF skins, item models through CustomModelData.
- **NPC conversations only in chat**, with clickable answers (`/trigger`). A datapack can't read what
  players type freely.
- **Quest rewards** can run commands secretly, for example to spawn an NPC or start a scene.
- Teleports, custom void dimensions, effects, sounds, titles, particles, simple scripted movement and timed
  sequences all work. 1.20.1 has no function macros, no `/random` and no mob size attribute (Pehkui
  covers size).
- Custom music is possible (an .ogg through a resource pack).
- A real custom mob or block would need a Forge/GeckoLib mod (planned as an optional "phase 5").
