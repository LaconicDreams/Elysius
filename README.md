# Elysius
An elytra survival modpack for Minecraft 1.20.1 (Forge).** The sun has gone dark. You wake up with a pair of wings that won't break, a logbook that isn't yours, and a world full of the dead. Fly across a huge reworked world, hunt bosses in ruins, sky towers and a dimension of their own, and fight roaming zombie hordes with real guns.
